What this direction is betting on
  • A familiar, trustworthy patient-record dashboard is enough, and is the lowest-risk replacement for Notion plus a form.
  • Forms with standard controls keep logging predictable; chat is a secondary side panel, not the main interface.
  • Plain tone and visible criteria beat novelty. Cost: it adds no extra pull for adherence on its own. prototype
