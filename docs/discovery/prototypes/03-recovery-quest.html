Possible warning signs: get medical help

This app cannot tell you what is wrong. What you described needs a person to check it. Do not continue exercises until you have been assessed.

EMERGENCY: get help now

Call 999 or go to A&E now if you have any of these:

  • Swelling of the whole leg, with warmth or redness
  • Leg feels tight, looks pale or blue, or is tingling or numb
  • Sudden breathlessness or chest pain

Call 999

URGENT REVIEW: contact a clinician within 24 hours

  • Pain getting worse 48-72 hours after activity
  • New sharp pain in the calf or behind the heel
  • A visible gap, dent or lump in the calf or tendon
  • Cannot put weight on the leg

Ring your GP, physiotherapist or NHS 111. Do not take anti-inflammatory painkillers unless your clinician says so. Ask your clinician.

Call NHS 111
